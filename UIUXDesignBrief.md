# ZoneEdan — UI/UX Design Brief

**Project Name:** ZoneEdan
**Project Type:** Travel & Tourism Discovery Web Application
**Initial Market:** Sri Lanka 🇱🇰
**Design Focus:** Water, Nature, Peace & Affordable Travel
**Primary Platform:** Responsive Web
**Design Direction:** Editorial Travel + Tropical Nature + Modern Minimalism
**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Design Overview

ZoneEdan is a travel discovery platform focused on helping users find beautiful, peaceful, and water-related destinations throughout Sri Lanka.

The visual experience should communicate:

* 🌊 Water
* 🌿 Nature
* 😌 Peace
* 🏝️ Escape
* 💰 Affordability
* 🇱🇰 Sri Lankan identity

The interface should feel less like a conventional travel booking platform and more like a **digital travel journal combined with a modern destination discovery platform**.

---

# 2. Core Design Concept

## "Find Your Little Eden"

The central visual idea behind ZoneEdan is:

> A user opens the website and feels like they have just discovered a peaceful corner of Sri Lanka.

The design should create a sense of:

```text
Busy World
     ↓
Open ZoneEdan
     ↓
Slow Down
     ↓
Explore
     ↓
Discover
     ↓
Escape
```

The interface should therefore avoid feeling overly corporate, crowded, or aggressively commercial.

---

# 3. Design Personality

ZoneEdan's personality should be:

### 🌊 Calm

Soft transitions, generous spacing, natural colors.

### 🌿 Natural

Organic shapes, landscape photography, earthy accents.

### ✨ Curious

Encourage users to discover lesser-known destinations.

### 💚 Warm

Friendly language and inviting interactions.

### 🧭 Adventurous

Subtle hints of exploration without becoming visually aggressive.

### 💰 Honest

Budget information should be clear and transparent.

# 8. Color Philosophy

The application should not use teal everywhere.

Recommended balance:

```text
Cream / White
████████████████████  50%

Natural Greens / Teals
████████████          30%

Sand / Warm Accents
██████                15%

Strong Accent Colors
██                     5%
```

The purpose is to create a calm visual environment rather than a saturated interface.

Important interactions should be thumb-friendly.

# 50. Accessibility-Friendly Color Usage

Never communicate information through color alone.

For example:

Bad:

```text
Green = Budget
Red = Expensive
```

Better:

```text
💰 Budget Friendly
```

combined with a label and visual styling.

---

# 51. Content Tone

ZoneEdan copy should be short, warm, and descriptive.

### Good

> A quiet stretch of coast where turquoise water meets golden sand.

### Avoid

> This destination is very beautiful and has many facilities for tourists.

The writing should create a sense of place.

---

# 52. Destination Description Style

Descriptions should answer:

* What does it feel like?
* What makes it special?
* What can I do there?
* Why should I go?

Example:

> **Diyaluma isn't just a waterfall — it's a place where the landscape opens up around you. Spend the day following the water, exploring natural pools, and taking in the hills of Badulla.**

---

# 53. Photography Direction

        ↓

        ↓

---

# 62. Design Do's

### DO

* Use large, beautiful photography.
* Use generous whitespace.
* Use expressive typography.
* Keep the interface calm.
* Make budget information obvious.
* Use natural colors.
* Create visual storytelling.
* Use subtle motion.
* Prioritize mobile usability.
* Let Sri Lankan landscapes be the star.

---

# 63. Design Don'ts

### DON'T

* Use excessive gradients.
* Use too many colors.
* Overuse rounded cards.
* Fill every section with cards.
* Use generic travel imagery.
* Hide budget information.
* Overuse animations.
* Make the UI look like a hotel booking platform.
* Use tiny text.
* Add features simply because they look impressive.

---

The final ZoneEdan interface should feel like:

> **A quiet morning beside a Sri Lankan waterfall.**

> A crowded tourism marketplace.

**ZoneEdan — Find Your Little Eden. 🌊🇱🇰**
