# ZoneEdan — Project Requirement Document

**Project Name:** ZoneEdan
**Project Type:** Travel & Tourism Discovery Web Application
**Initial Market:** Sri Lanka 🇱🇰
**Development Model:** Solo, Part-Time
**Frontend:** React + Tailwind CSS
**Backend:** Supabase
**Primary Theme:** Water, Nature, Peace & Affordable Travel

---

## 1. Product Overview

ZoneEdan is a travel and tourism discovery platform designed to help people discover the most beautiful, peaceful, and water-related destinations in Sri Lanka while keeping their travel budget in mind.

The name **ZoneEdan** combines the idea of a personal **“Zone”** with **“Eden”**, representing a peaceful and beautiful place where people can temporarily escape from their busy everyday lives.

ZoneEdan is not intended to be a conventional hotel or flight booking platform.

Instead, it focuses on:

* Destination discovery
* Peaceful travel experiences
* Water-based destinations
* Budget-friendly travel
* Practical destination information
* Curated travel inspiration

### Core Product Promise

> **Find Your Little Eden.**

---

# 2. Vision

To create a beautiful and trustworthy travel discovery platform that helps people find peaceful water destinations in Sri Lanka without assuming that memorable travel has to be expensive.

ZoneEdan aims to make discovering a destination feel like discovering a hidden escape.

---

# 3. Mission

ZoneEdan's mission is to:

* Make beautiful destinations easier to discover.
* Promote peaceful and nature-focused travel.
* Highlight Sri Lanka's water-related destinations.
* Make approximate travel costs visible.
* Help users make better-informed travel decisions.
* Encourage exploration beyond overcrowded tourist locations.

---

# 4. Problem Statement

Travelers often discover destinations through multiple disconnected sources such as:

* Social media
* YouTube
* Travel blogs
* Google searches
* Booking websites
* Personal recommendations

While these platforms provide useful information, travelers may still struggle to answer:

* Where should I go?
* Is the place peaceful?
* Is it worth visiting?
* How much might the trip cost?
* What kind of water experience does it offer?
* How long should I stay?
* What activities are available?
* Is it suitable for a couple, family, friends, or solo travel?
* When is the best time to visit?

ZoneEdan aims to bring these discovery-focused answers into one simple experience.

# 9. Unique Product Features

## 9.1 ZoneEdan Peace Score

Each destination can have a **ZoneEdan Peace Score**.

Example:

> **Peace Score: 4.7 / 5**

The score can consider curated factors such as:

* Crowd level
* Noise
* Natural surroundings
* Water environment
* Overall escape feeling

The score is an editorial ZoneEdan metric and should not be presented as an objective scientific measurement.

---

## 9.2 Budget-Friendly Discovery

Each destination should provide an estimated budget range.

Example:

> **Estimated Trip Cost**
> LKR 8,000 – 15,000

Possible cost categories:

* Transportation
* Accommodation
* Food
* Activities
* Miscellaneous expenses

Prices should always be presented as estimates because real travel costs can change.

## 9.3 Water-Based Discovery

# 16. Budget Model

Budget information is one of ZoneEdan's key features.

Each destination should have an estimated budget range.

Example:

```text
Destination: Mirissa

Transportation: LKR 2,000 – 4,000
Stay:           LKR 3,000 – 6,000
Food:           LKR 1,500 – 3,000
Activities:     LKR 1,000 – 2,000

Estimated Total:
LKR 7,500 – 15,000
```

The application should clearly state:

> Prices are estimates and may vary depending on season, transportation, accommodation, and personal spending.

The system must allow users to filter destinations using supported filters.

# 25. Product Constraints

Because ZoneEdan is a solo part-time project:

* The MVP must remain manageable.
* Features should be implemented incrementally.
* Backend complexity should be introduced only when needed.

* New features should be evaluated against the core product purpose.
